Education and Awards



Robert Griffiths QC, Head of Chambers at Mondial

…

 Attended Haverfordwest Grammar School

…

 Played cricket for Glamoran second XI (batsman)

…

Awarded an open scholarship (history) to read Jurisprudence at St Edmund Hall, Oxford

…

Called to bar in 1974, appointed Queen’s Cousel in 1993

…

Member of the MCC Cricket committee on and off since 2001

…

Trustee of Lord’s Taverners since 2011 (2010-2013); has focussed on the enhancement of sporting facilities and opportunities for the disadvantaged

…

A Freeman of the City of London and a Liveryman with the Worshipful Company of Fan Makers

…

Has appeared in a large number of leading cases and acted for widely differing clients such as the Sultan of Brunei, Bucks Fizz, The Who and Heathrow land owners

…

 Has wide international experience; Senior Counsel in New South Wales and ad hoc admissions to Bars of Brunei, Hong Kong, Kuala Lumpur, Singapore and West Malaysia

…

Memeber of Council and Director of London Chamber of Commerce and Industry since 2014

…

Chairman of the regulatory board of the Greyhound racing board for Great Britain

…

Member of Middlesex CCC and the Garrick Club
